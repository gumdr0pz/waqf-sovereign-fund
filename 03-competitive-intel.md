## Regulatory Gaps & First-Mover Advantage

1. **No country** has established a sovereign waqf investment vehicle with institutional-grade governance
2. **Malaysia** has the most complete regulatory stack (SC, BNM, JAWHAR, state enactments) — closest to deployable
3. **Indonesia** has scale (62 banks, largest Muslim population) but regulatory fragmentation
4. **Turkey** has historical precedent (Vakıfbank since 1954) but no explicit waqf-investment mandate
5. **Saudi** has PIF scale but no shariah-structured SWF model

**Bottom line**: First-mover to establish a **legally sovereign, shariah-binding, professionally managed waqf fund** would capture enormous brand, religious, and economic authority in the Islamic finance ecosystem.
