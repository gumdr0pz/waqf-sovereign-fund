# WAQF SOVEREIGN FUND — Global Landscape & Architecture

## BLUF
No established "waqf sovereign fund" class exists yet. The field is massively underexploited.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Global SWFs tracked | ~400+ (incl. public pension funds) |
| Explicitly Islamic / Waqf SWFs | Near zero |
| Micro Waqf Banks | 62 (Indonesia, microfinance, not sovereign) |
| Global waqf asset value | $100B – $1T (mostly idle) |
| Malaysia waqf assets | RM1.3T (~$300B USD), 87% undeveloped |

---

## 1. Global Count

### A. "Waqf Banks" — Limited & Niche
- **Indonesia**: 62 Micro Waqf Banks (~49,700 customers). Microfinance institutions, not sovereign vehicles.
- **Malaysia**: myWakaf initiative (Bank Negara). Collaboration framework between Islamic banks and SIRCs, not standalone.
- **No other countries** have deployed a formal "waqf bank" at scale.

### B. "Islamic Sovereign Wealth Funds" (ISWFs) — Theoretical
- **Zero** formally branded or structured waqf-based ISWFs exist.
- Existing SWFs from Muslim countries (Saudi PIF, Qatar, UAE, Khazanah, Brunei) operate on **conventional SWF architecture**.
- **400+ SWFs globally** exist; waqf is a $100B–$1T asset class sitting mostly frozen.

### C. Waqf-Featured Funds (Malaysia Hybrid)
- Malaysia's SC launched Waqf-Featured Fund Framework for unit trusts/wholesale funds — closest regulatory bridge.
- Investment funds with waqf features, not sovereign funds.

## 2. Core Architecture

### Malaysia (Most Advanced)

| Layer | Entity | Role |
|-------|--------|------|
| State Level | SIRC | Owns waqf land/assets; statutory authority |
| Federal Coordination | JAWHAR | Policy, data, central registry |
| Banking Partnership | Islamic Banks (AIBIM/BNM) | myWakaf — fund collection, investment mgmt |
| Capital Market | SC | Waqf-Featured Fund Framework |
| Shariah Compliance | SAC / Internal Committees | Rulings, compliance oversight |

**Key**: All waqf is **state-level jurisdiction** (13 states + FT). No single national waqf fund.

### Hassan & Bahoo (2019) — 4-Pillar ISWF Model

| Pillar | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| 1. Major Shariah Principles | Riba-free, gharar-free, asset-backed. Mudarabah/musharakah, not interest. |
| 2. Islamic Corporate Governance | Independent Shariah board with veto. Hisbah. Shura. Accountability to ummah. |
| 3. Islamic Transparency & Disclosure | Beyond IFRS: social impact, zakat, beneficiary reach. Open-book on asset utilization. |
| 4. Islamic CSR | Mandatory community reinvestment. Education, healthcare, infrastructure. SDG-aligned, maqasid-filtered. |

## 3. Why None Exist Yet

| Barrier | Assessment |
|---------|------------|
| Jurisdictional Fragmentation | Waqf is state-level (Malaysia), local (Indonesia), or unregistered. No centralized asset base for "sovereign" vehicle. |
| Idle Assets | 87% of Malaysia's RM1.3T waqf is undeveloped land. Requires development financing. |
| Governance Gap | State Religious Councils are administrative bodies, not investment-grade fund managers. |
| Regulatory Bridge Missing | Conventional SWF law ≠ waqf law. Requires special act or central bank framework. |
| Political Economy | Sovereign funds = state power. Waqf = religious endowment. Tension exists. |

---

## 4. Architecture Options to Build One

### Option A: Islamic Sovereign Waqf Fund (ISWF)
- National-level fund endowed by state waqf land + cash waqf collections
- Managed by professional fund manager under Shariah board
- Returns split: reinvestment (growth) + beneficiaries (social spend)
- Requires: **Special Waqf Sovereign Fund Act**

### Option B: Waqf-Featuring SWF (Malaysia-style)
- Conventional SWF dedicating a waqf-portion of assets
- Unit trust / wholesale fund structure (already legal in Malaysia)
- Easier path; less sovereignty

### Option C: Waqf Investment Bank
- Full-fledged Islamic bank with waqf mandate
- Intermediary: collects cash waqf → invests → distributes
- Indonesia's Micro Waqf Banks are baby-steps
