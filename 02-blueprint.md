## 1. Legal Structure

### Option A: Statutory Fund (Recommended)
- Established by **Special Act of Parliament**
- Separate legal personality from state and religious councils
- Government guarantee + independent governance
- Example models: Khazanah Malaysia Act, Temasek Act

### Option B: Trust Structure
- Established under **Waqf (State) Enactment**
- State Religious Council as settlor/trustee
- Professional fund manager as investment agent
- Weaker for cross-border/capital market access

### Option C: Islamic Investment Bank + Waqf Arm
- Licensed under **Islamic Financial Services Act (IFSA) / Central Bank**
- Separate waqf investment division with profit-sharing
- Easier regulatory path; less "sovereign" branding

## 2. Governance Architecture

### 3-Tier Model

| Tier | Body | Composition | Powers |
|------|------|-------------|--------|
| **Tier 1: Ultimate Authority** | Waqf Sovereign Fund Council (WSFC) | State Rulers + Federal reps + Shariah scholars + Independent directors | Charter amendment, Shariah policy, auditor appointment |
| **Tier 2: Investment Authority** | Investment Board | Investment professionals, ESG/Impact experts, Islamic finance specialists | Capital allocation, fund manager selection, mandate definition |
| **Tier 3: Independent Oversight** | Shariah Advisory Board (SAB) + Internal Audit + Hisbah Auditor | Fiqh scholars + forensic accountants | **Veto power** on non-compliant investments, hisbah investigations, public disclosure |

### Shariah Governance (Beyond Conventional)
1. **SAB** issues binding rulings on all investments (not advisory)
2. **Hisbah Auditor** verifies social impact + distribution equity
3. **Public Accountability Council** — citizen/ummah representatives with right to audit
4. Zakat computation + distribution is **mandatory audit item**

## 3. Capital Structure

### Sources
| Source | Type | Mechanism |
|--------|------|-----------|
| State Waqf Land | Endowment (in-kind) | Land transfer to fund; development via lease/ijarah |
| Cash Waqf | Endowment (cash) | Public donations + institutional subscriptions |

### Asset Mix Target
| Asset Class | Target | Rationale |
|-------------|--------|-----------|
| Real Estate / Waqf Land Development | 30-40% | Core endowment, ijarah income, community benefit |
| Islamic Equities (halal-screened) | 20-30% | Growth, dividend income |
| Sukuks | 20-25% | Stable income, capital preservation |
| Private Equity (Islamic/Impact) | 10-15% | High growth, job creation, technology |
| Gold / Commodities | 5-10% | Inflation hedge, shariah-compliant store of value |
| Cash / FX | 5% | Liquidity buffer |

## 4. Investment Mandate

### Primary Mandate
1. **Preserve capital** of waqf endowment in perpetuity
2. **Generate sustainable income** for beneficiaries
3. **Develop waqf assets** for community benefit
4. **Support Islamic finance ecosystem** growth

### Restrictions
- No riba (interest-bearing instruments)
- No gharar (excessive uncertainty/derivatives)
- No haram sectors (alcohol, tobacco, gambling, conventional finance, weapons)
- No speculative short-selling
- Screens: debt-to-assets, interest income thresholds (e.g., 33%)

### Impact Overlay
All investments scored against **Maqasid al-Shariah**:
1. Preservation of faith
2. Preservation of life
3. Preservation of intellect
4. Preservation of lineage
5. Preservation of wealth
6. Preservation of environment (contemporary extension)

## 7. Phased Implementation

### Phase 1: Foundation (0-12 months)
- Secure enabling legislation / special act
- Assemble WSFC + SAB
- Transfer initial state waqf land to fund
- Set up legal entity, bank accounts, custodian

### Phase 2: Mobilization (12-24 months)
- Launch cash waqf collection platform
- First sukuk issuance (infrastructure)
- Seed investment team + fund manager
- Publish charter + investment policy statement

### Phase 3: Scale (24-48 months)
- Full asset allocation across all target classes
- Cross-border waqf partnerships (OIC, IDB, AAOIFI)
- Public dashboard + blockchain transparency live
- Annual impact report + beneficiary audit

### Phase 4: Institutional (48+ months)
- Benchmarked vs conventional SWFs (return + impact)
- Model exported to other Muslim-majority countries
- Waqf REIT / ETF products for retail investors
- Sovereign credibility established
